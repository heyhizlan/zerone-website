The company

SEC-01 / COMPANY

ZerOne is an established automobile performance parts company based in Kuala Lumpur, operating as FT Innovative Sdn Bhd (636002-T). The business runs from a large office, warehouse and distribution facility rather than a shopfront — the parts are engineered, stocked and shipped from one place.

That facility supplies ZerOne's own dealer network across six states, holding the stock and the technical knowledge dealers draw on when they set a car up. It is also where the standard in section 02 gets enforced, part by part, before anything ships.

02

Standards

The line ZerOne does not cross.

SEC-02 / STANDARDS

ZerOne produces and supplies only performance parts of the highest quality.

01 / Quality floor

Highest quality only. Every part ZerOne produces or supplies is held to that standard — there is no lower tier of the same product.

02 / No second-hand

Second-hand parts are not sold. What leaves the facility is new, full stop.

03 / No exceptions

Inferior or low-standard parts are refused. If it does not meet the standard, it does not ship as a ZerOne part.

04

Engineering

ADFL, built for the roads it runs on.

SEC-04 / TECHNOLOGY

ADFL — Active Damping Force Levelling — is the automatic variable valve at the centre of the SSR700 range. It was engineered with Mr. Kichiro, a technical engineer from Japan, and tuned specifically for Malaysian road conditions rather than adapted from a system built for somewhere else.

The valve varies using hydraulic oil pressure, automatically raising or reducing damping force as road surface and driving style change — holding steering response, roadability and traction across the full adjustment range.
