02

What to ask your dealer

Bring these and the visit is five minutes shorter.

SEC-02 / BRIEFING
01

The car and variant

Make, model, generation and engine/variant — fitment is model-specific, so this is the first thing they will need.

02

Current and intended ride height

What it sits at now, and how much lower (or not) you actually want it — this sets the spring rate and the body-shift position.

03

Tyre and wheel setup

Sizes fitted now, and any change planned — offset and rolling diameter both affect what height and damping suit the car.

04

Daily-driven or track use

How the car is actually used, not how you would like to use it — a daily and a track-day car are set up differently.

05

What you want changed

What the car does today that you want different — too soft, too harsh, rolls in corners, unsettled over bumps. Be specific.
