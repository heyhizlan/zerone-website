SEC-01 / POSITION

A damper is not a part you fit and forget. It is a range you set — and the range is the whole point.

ZerOne has built performance suspension out of Kuala Lumpur for more than two decades. Coilovers, sport absorbers, sport springs and chassis bracing — engineered, assembled and supported here, and tuned against the surfaces Malaysian cars actually run on rather than a European test loop.

Every kit in the range is adjustable in more than one axis. Ride height with body-shift so you keep full stroke. Damping force in discrete, repeatable clicks. On the SSR700, an ADFL valve that keeps adjusting after you have stopped.

ADFL

Active Damping Force Levelling.

SEC-03 / TECHNOLOGY

The damper
keeps adjusting
after you stop.

ADFL is an automatic variable valve. It reads hydraulic oil pressure and varies the valve continuously — raising or dropping damping force with the road surface and the way the car is being driven, across the whole adjustment range rather than at one setting.

Steering response, roadability and traction hold up on a surface that changes under the car. The system was engineered with Japanese damper engineer Mr. Kichiro and tuned specifically against Malaysian road conditions.
