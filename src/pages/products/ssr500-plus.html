Two axes of adjustment is where a car stops being set for you and starts being set by you.

  1. 01
    Upper mount

    Pillow Ball Upper Mount is available across the ZerOne range — confirm what your kit ships with.

  2. 02
    Rebound adjustment

    Sets how the damper controls the return stroke — the axis that governs how settled the car feels over a crest.

  3. 03
    Compression adjustment

    Sets resistance on the bump stroke. Adjusted to the surface and to how the car is being driven.

  4. 04
    Damper body

    Construction type is not published for this kit — confirm with your dealer.

  5. 05
    Spring and seat

    Spring specification and rate are set per car. Confirm with your dealer before ordering.
