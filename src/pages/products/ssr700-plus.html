01

Anatomy

Six parts of the SSR700 PLUS, top of the strut downward. Hover a row to isolate it.

SEC-01 / ANATOMY
01 02 03 04 05 06

Every adjustment on this kit is mechanical and repeatable — indexed, not guessed.

  1. 01
    Pillow ball upper mount

    Spherical bearing at the top of the strut. Listed in the ZerOne accessory range — confirm what your kit ships with.

  2. 02
    32-step compression adjuster

    Sets resistance on the bump stroke. Thirty-two indexed positions, each one repeatable.

  3. 03
    32-step rebound adjuster

    Controls the return stroke on its own axis, independent of the compression setting.

  4. 04
    Mono-tube damper body

    Single-tube construction with a larger piston diameter. ADFL varies the valve inside it using hydraulic oil pressure.

  5. 05
    Carbon thread casing · Hi/Lo collars

    Ride height and spring preload set on separate collars, so lowering the car does not spend the damper's stroke.

  6. 06
    SAE9254 coil spring

    Cold-coiled chromium alloy. Rate is specified per car — your dealer sets it.

04

ADFL

Active Damping Force Levelling.

SEC-04 / TECHNOLOGY

The damper keeps
adjusting after
you stop.

ADFL is an automatic variable valve. It varies the valve using hydraulic oil pressure, raising or reducing damping force according to road conditions and driving style — across any damping force range, not at one setting.

Steering response, roadability and traction hold up on a surface that changes under the car. Engineered by Mr. Kichiro, technical engineer, Japan, and tuned for Malaysian road conditions.
