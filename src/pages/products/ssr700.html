SSR Series / Mono-tube

SSR700 Mono-tube

A mono-tube damper with a larger piston diameter, thirty-two steps of compression and rebound, Hi/Lo height by body-shift and an ADFL valve that keeps working after the dial has stopped. For aggressive handling on street and track — sporty, and still comfortable.

01

Anatomy

Six parts of the SSR700, top of the strut downward. Hover a row to isolate it.

SEC-01 / ANATOMY
01 02 03 04 05 06

One tube, one gas chamber, and a valve that reads the road while you drive.

  1. 01
    Pillow ball upper mount

    Spherical bearing at the top of the strut. Listed in the ZerOne accessory range — confirm what your kit ships with.

  2. 02
    32-step damping adjuster

    Compression and rebound across thirty-two indexed positions. Every setting is repeatable by number.

  3. 03
    ADFL valve

    Automatic variable valve. Varies damping force with hydraulic oil pressure as the surface changes.

  4. 04
    Mono-tube damper body

    Single-tube construction with a larger piston diameter.

  5. 05
    Carbon thread casing · Hi/Lo collars

    Height and spring preload run on separate collars — body-shift, so lowering the car does not spend damper stroke.

  6. 06
    SAE9254 coil spring

    Cold-coiled chromium alloy. Rate is specified per car — your dealer sets it.
